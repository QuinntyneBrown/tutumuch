# Tilly: artwork and attribution

Tilly is TuTuMuch's character from the TuTuMuch brand kit. These WebP files are copies of the
cut-out poses in `docs/print/characters/`, scaled to 600 px tall with the alpha kept, for pages
(ADR general/0009). See [the full attribution](../../../../../../docs/print/characters/ATTRIBUTION.md) for the source images and poses.
