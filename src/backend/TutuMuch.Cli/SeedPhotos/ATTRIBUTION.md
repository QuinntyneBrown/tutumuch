# Seed photo attribution

Every photo in this folder is a photograph by the author named below, published on Flickr under
the [Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 Generic licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/)
(CC BY 2.0) and distributed in the [Open Images Dataset V4](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/index.html),
which lists each image's licence, author and original page. The photos are not covered by the
repository's MIT licence; they keep their CC BY 2.0 licence. See
[ADR platform/0010](../../../../docs/adr/platform/0010-tutumuch-cli-for-demo-seeding.md).

**Changes:** each photo was taken from the Open Images copy (longest side 1024 px), then either
padded to a square with its own background colour ("padded") or centre-cropped to a square
("cropped"), resized to 1024 × 1024 and re-saved as JPEG at quality 82. `navy-board-shorts.jpg`
also had the caption strip at the bottom removed. The seeder uploads them through the photo
pipeline, which strips metadata and re-encodes them again.

Brand names on seeded listings come from `Selling:SuggestedBrands` and do not describe the
item in the photo. No photo is endorsed by its author or by any brand.
