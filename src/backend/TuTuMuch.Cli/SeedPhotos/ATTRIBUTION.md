# Seed photos

Every image in this folder is an original illustration generated for TuTuMuch: a flat, pastel
product-style drawing of a girls' item on a soft background, rendered as a 1024 × 1024 JPEG at
quality 82. There is one folder per category, named exactly as the category (`Clothing`, `Shoes`,
`Dance`, `Sports`, `Cameras and tech`, `Toys and crafts`), and each demo listing uses photos from
its own category's folder. No stock photography or third-party image is used, so the images are
covered by the repository's licence like the rest of the code. See
[ADR platform/0010](../../../../docs/adr/platform/0010-tutumuch-cli-for-demo-seeding.md).

The seeder uploads them through the photo pipeline, which strips metadata and re-encodes them
again. Brand names on seeded listings come from `Selling:SuggestedBrands` and do not describe the
item in the illustration; no brand endorses or is depicted by any image.
