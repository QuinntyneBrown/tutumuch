Overview

Parents browse and sell from their phones, often with a child on one arm. Every screen is designed at 320–360px first, then gains columns at the L2-061 breakpoints.

Touch

  • Primary actions ≥ 44px; everything ≥ 24px.
  • Hover-only affordances are always visible on (hover: none).
  • Fixed bottom bars add env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • Use 100dvh for full-height layouts.

Testing

  • Screenshot at 320, 360×800, 768×1024, 992 and 1280×800 in both themes, keyboard only.
  • No horizontal page scroll at 320px (L2-061 AC1); dialogs become sheets; nav collapses into the menu sheet.
