Foundation · Giddy

Shape

Round and soft: pill buttons, 20px cards and photos, 28px sheets. Nothing is square-cut.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableGiddy design system

Overview

Radius grows with size: 8px compact, 14px inputs, 20px cards and dialogs, 28px sheets and heroes, full pills for buttons, chips, badges and avatars.

Radii

--radius-xs
--radius-sm
--radius-md
--radius-lg
--radius-xl
--radius-full

Borders

  • 1px hairline for edges and dividers.
  • 2px for focus rings and the selected gallery thumbnail.

Usage

  • Buttons, chips, badges, the size tag, photo stamps, switches and avatars are pills.
  • Listing photos and cards use --radius-lg; thumbnails --radius-md; dialogs --radius-lg and bottom sheets --radius-xl at the top.
  • Nested shapes use a smaller radius than their container.
  • Never cut corners with clip-path; every shape comes from a radius token.
