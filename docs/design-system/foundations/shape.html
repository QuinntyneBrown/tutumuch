Shape

Soft, rounded shapes: 2rem panels, 1.5rem cards and images, 1rem inputs, and pills for buttons, chips and badges.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablePage guidelines
  • Overview
  • Radii
  • Borders
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Shape follows the page guidelines (section 7). Four radii carry the look; nothing is square-cut and nothing is clipped.

ShapeTokenValueUsed by
Large panels--radius-panel (= --radius-xl)2remDialogs, sheets, the marketing hero, category headers
Cards and images--radius-card (= --radius-lg)1.5remListing photos, cards, toasts, panels
Inputs and swatches--radius-input (= --radius-md)1remText fields, selects, wells
Pills--radius-full9999pxButtons, chips, badges, the size tag, condition badges, avatars

Borders

  • Group with spacing, headings and thin ink/10 lines (--color-border-default), not bordered cards.
  • 1px hairline for separators; 2px for the secondary button ring and the focus ring.
  • Form controls keep a strong edge (--color-border-strong, at least 3:1).

Usage

  • Nested shapes use a smaller radius than their container; thumbnails use --radius-sm.
  • Bottom sheets round their top corners with --radius-panel.
  • Never cut corners with clip-path; every shape comes from a radius token.
