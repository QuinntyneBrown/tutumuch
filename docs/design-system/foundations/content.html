Content

Warm, short and confident: a friendly mom at the team picnic, not a corporate brand. Speak to the parent; let Tilly speak to the girl.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablePage guidelines

Overview

Copy follows the page guidelines (section 5). Celebrate girls’ activity and confidence (“built for practice”, “ready for the recital”, “goals left in them”) and be honest about condition: never oversell a used item.

Voice and tone

  • Warm: “What is she passing on?”
  • Short: “The closet is empty for now.”
  • Confident: “Every listing is for girls.”
  • Reassuring in trouble: “Your draft is safe. Try again in a minute.”
  • Tilly’s voice, for the girl, in marketing and empty moments only; her catchphrase “Never too much tutu!” at most once.

Mechanics

  • Sentence case for everything except names and brands; no letterspaced capitals.
  • Buttons are verbs: “Shop the closet”, “Sell outgrown gear”, “Buy now”, “Make an offer”. Never OK, Submit or Yes.
  • No exclamation points in UI labels. At most one per page, and only in Tilly’s voice.
  • No emoji.
  • Don’t write “AI”, “curated” or “elevate”.
  • Prices show cents only when non-zero in grids (“$32”), always at checkout (“$18.00”).
  • Use “·” to join facts in meta lines: “Girls · Used - Excellent”.

Standard terms

ConceptTermNote
CatalogThe closet“Shop the closet”, “Search the closet”. Routes and page titles stay.
SellingPass it on / Sell outgrown gearThe top bar link stays “Sell”.
ConditionThe domain labels: New with tags, New without tags, Used - Excellent, Used - Good, Used - FairThe guideline scale (New with tags · Like new · Gently loved · Well loved) sets the badge colour; renaming the labels is deferred.
SizeGirls’Every listing is Girls’; the size tag reads “Girls”. Numeric sizes are deferred.
CartBagOnly where a bag exists. TuTuMuch has no cart today, so there is no Bag copy.

Formulas

  • Error: what happened and what to do, in plain language. “Couldn’t load the closet. Try again.”
  • Field error, next to the field: the fix. “Enter a price like 18.00.”
  • Empty: Tilly, one short line and one action. “Nothing listed yet” → Sell outgrown gear.
  • Success: a short toast. “Listing published.”
  • Sold: “Sold” and “Find similar”.
