Foundation · Giddy

Content

Warm, clear and brief. Talk like a helpful parent at the school gate who knows a good hand-me-down.

Overview

TuTuMuch writes for busy parents in Giddy’s voice: lead with the answer, use real names, sizes and prices, and keep it light without being silly. Fun lives in the colours, the tutu and an occasional warm word at a milestone, not in every sentence.

Voice and tone

  • Friendly: “What are you selling?”
  • Specific: “Size: Girls’. Every listing on TuTuMuch is for girls.”
  • Reassuring in trouble: “Your draft is safe. Try again in a minute.”
  • Warm at milestones: “Your listing is live.”

Mechanics

  • Sentence case for everything except names and brands.
  • Buttons are verbs: “Sell”, “Buy now”, “Publish listing”. Never OK/Submit/Yes.
  • Dates: “Fri 30 Oct”, “in 2 days”; prices show cents: “$18.00”.
  • Sizes read “Girls’”; the size tag reads “Girls”.
  • Use “·” to join facts in meta lines.

Formulas

  • Error: what happened + what’s safe + what to do. “Couldn’t load the catalog. Try again.”
  • Field error: the fix. “Enter a price like 18.00.”
  • Empty: what this is + why empty + one action. “Nothing for sale yet. List an item to see it here.”
  • Success: what happened + what happens next.
