Iconography

Line icons from tl-icon on a 24-unit grid with 2px round strokes in currentColor. Icon-only buttons are always labelled.

Overview

Icons come from tl-icon (<tl-icon name="search" />): inline SVG on a 24-unit grid with 2px round strokes in currentColor, the stand-in for the kit’s lucide-react. Pictures come from listing photos, the brand art comes from tl-logo, tl-mark and Tilly; there is no emoji art. The favicon and app icon are the Tilly face tile.

Sizes

16, 20 and 24px: .icon--sm, .icon (default) and .icon--lg. Error-tone empty states use a round well with a 24px icon; other empty states show Tilly.

Colour

Icons inherit the text colour. Error icons are raspberry (--color-danger-icon), never an all-red screen; each status icon uses its --color-*-icon token and always sits next to words.

Couldn’t load items

A raspberry error icon in its well.

Labelling

  • Decorative icons: aria-hidden="true".
  • Every icon-only button has an aria-label: “Open menu”, “Search”, “Dismiss”.
  • Meaningful standalone icons: role="img" and aria-label.
