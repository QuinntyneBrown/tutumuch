Foundation

Color

The TuTuMuch brand palette — raspberry, lilac, blush, sunny, cream, ink and muted — as semantic tokens, with a plum night dark theme. No gradients; every text pair passes WCAG 2.2 AA.

Overview

Colour follows the page guidelines (section 3) as mapped by ADR general/0009. The brand kit’s exact hex values live only in the primitives tier of tokens/tokens.css; components and pages use the semantic --color-* tokens and never a hex value. Colour has two jobs: brand (raspberry for the one primary action, links and active states; blush for soft sections; lilac for selection; sunny for highlights) and meaning (info, success, warning, danger, always with an icon and words).

  • No gradients. No blobs, glows or decorative shapes. The showcase panels are solid blush.
  • The only patterns are the brand polka dot and gingham, and only inside tl-logo and tl-mark.
  • Light: cream canvas, white surfaces, raspberry 600 accent with white labels, raspberry 700 links, ink text.
  • Dark: a plum night (plum 950 canvas, plum 900 surfaces), raspberry 400 accent with plum labels, raspberry 300 links.

Contrast rules

  • Body text is always ink or muted on cream, white or blush (on blush, secondary text uses --color-fg-subtle).
  • White text on raspberry only at 16px semibold or larger.
  • Never light text (white, blush, sunny) on a light background.
  • Raspberry 600 is 4.4:1 on cream, so raspberry text and links use raspberry 700 (--color-fg-link).

Logo and mark colours

The logo art keeps its own colours, used only inside tl-logo and tl-mark through the --color-logo-* and --color-mark-bg tokens, in both themes: lilac and lavender gingham, polka-dot pink, the ink outline and the raspberry shadow; the reversed tone (raspberry backgrounds only) uses an ink shadow, paler dots and a sunny “Much”. Usage

  • Lilac only for selection and small accents, never text on light.
  • Sunny sparingly, always with ink text.
  • A new theme overrides semantic tokens only, then extends contrast-pairs.json and reruns the checker.
