Foundation · Giddy

Motion

Quick, springy and purposeful. Everything stops for reduced motion except status loops.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableGiddy design system
  • Overview
  • Durations and easings
  • Reduced motion
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Motion explains where things come from: sheets slide up, dialogs scale in, toasts rise, listing photos zoom gently on hover. Hover is 120 ms, reveals 200 ms, dialogs 320 ms. Loops (spinner, shimmer) have their own tokens.

Durations and easings

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion the tokens set durations to 0.01 ms and components drop animations; skeleton shimmer and indeterminate bars become static. The spinner keeps one slow turn because it reports status. The Angular root also disables animations through @.disabled (L2-065 AC6).

Usage

  • Never animate on page load except skeleton shimmer.
  • Nothing over 500 ms except loops and progress.
  • Enter with --ease-enter, exit with --ease-exit; toasts slide up, dialogs scale in from 576px and slide up below it.
  • Spring easing is for playful lifts only, such as the primary button rising on hover.
