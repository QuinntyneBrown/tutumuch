Layout

Desktop-first pages on a standard skeleton: a two-row header, the H1, the primary content, at most one supporting section, and the footer. Content 72rem, product grids 80rem.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablePage guidelines

Overview

Layout follows the page guidelines (section 7). The app shell sets the cream canvas, DM Sans and ink text at full height. Content sits in .container (--layout-container-max, 72rem) with 24px side padding, 40px from 768px; product grids may use .container--wide (--layout-container-wide, 80rem). Design for desktop first and use the width.

Standard page skeleton

  1. Header (Top bar): logo left (the mark under 640px), search in the middle, Sell and Account on the right; the six categories in a second row from 768px. No Bag: there is no cart.
  2. Page H1 with an optional one-line description.
  3. Primary content: the thing the user came for.
  4. One supporting section at most.
  5. Footer (Footer): the mark, short links, “© TuTuMuch”.

Grids

Product grids are 4 columns from 992px, 3 from 768px and 2 below, never 1 (tl-listing-grid). The page grid tokens go from 4 to 8 columns at 768px and 12 at 1024px.

123456789101112

Resize the window: columns, gutters and margins follow the tokens.

Sections and grouping

  • Section spacing --space-16, --space-20 from 768px.
  • Separate sections with spacing or a background change (white or blush), not borders.
  • Don’t wrap everything in bordered cards; group with spacing, headings and thin ink/10 lines.
  • Body text keeps to about 70 characters (--layout-measure).

Breakpoints

WidthWhat changes
Below 640pxThe header shows the mark in the home link
640pxThe wordmark returns
768pxCategory row in the header, 3-column grids, larger H1 and body, 40px margins
992pxFilters sidebar, 4-column grids, two-column marketing hero
1200pxWidest photos

The --layout-breakpoint-* tokens hold the reference values; media queries are written in rem because custom properties cannot be used there.

Shells

  • App shell: skip link, top bar, main#main.container.page, footer, toast region.
  • Auth shell: the large logo above a centred card.
  • Admin shell: top bar with the Admin tag, no footer.
  • Bare shell: logo-only top bar for maintenance and offline pages.
  • Marketing shell: full-bleed bands (hero, feature bands) instead of the container.
