Listing card

One listing in a grid: photo first, the title on one line, the brand, “Girls · condition”, and the price pushed to the bottom so rows line up.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

tl-listing-grid lays out tl-listing-cards in 2 columns below 768px, 3 from 768px and 4 from 992px, never 1. The card shows only what helps a parent decide at a glance. The whole card is one link to the listing. Every card fills its grid row and pushes its price row down with margin-top: auto, so prices line up across the row.

Anatomy

  1. Photo4:5 white well, --radius-card (1.5rem), cover-fitted, explicit width and height
  2. TitleOne line, truncated, 16px medium
  3. BrandMuted, 14px; kept because L2-022 requires it
  4. Size · condition“Girls · Used - Excellent”, muted, 14px
  5. PriceFraunces 20px, tabular figures, pushed down

Sizes

The card fills its grid column. The photo sizes attribute is (min-width: 1200px) 270px, (min-width: 992px) 23vw, (min-width: 768px) 31vw, 50vw, so a 375px phone loads the 400w version (L2-059).

States

Pink tulle tutu skirtJanie and JackGirls · Used - Excellent$32
Loading: blush skeleton cards shaped like the card, in a grid with aria-busy="true" (see Skeleton). Hover underlines the title; the photo does not zoom. Focus shows the ring around the link.

Responsive behaviour

Columns change at 48rem and 62rem. Product grids may use .container--wide (80rem). The first 4 cards load eagerly; the rest are lazy.

Theming

The photo well stays white in both themes (--color-photo-bg), like a real product photo. No card shadow and no border: cards are grouped by the grid gap.

Accessibility

The grid is a ul role="list". The link’s name is the photo’s alt text (“Photo 1 of {brand} {title}”) followed by the visible text. A Sold listing says “Sold” in text, not only by the greyed photo.

Meta on canvas
Title on canvas

Content

  • Title as the seller wrote it, one line: “Pink tulle tutu skirt”.
  • Size · condition reads “Girls · {condition}” with the domain condition label.
  • Prices are whole dollars without cents (“$32”), cents only when non-zero (“$18.50”).
  • No heart and no original price: saved hearts and original prices do not exist yet (deferred in ADR general/0009).

Do and don't

  • Don’t add buttons inside the card; it is one link.
  • Don’t put Tilly next to listing photos.
