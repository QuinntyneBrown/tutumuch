Tulle field

A decorative canvas of translucent tulle petals behind the marketing hero; they drift up and twirl around the pointer.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.1

Overview

tl-tulle-field is the one decorative layer allowed on any page (ADR general/0010). It sits in the [backdrop] slot of tl-marketing-hero on the Phase 1 marketing page, behind the copy, Tilly and the poster.

Anatomy

  1. A full-bleed canvas, absolutely positioned behind the hero content (z-index: 0)
  2. Petals: scalloped fans, like one layer of a tutu seen from above, at 18 to 50% opacity by depth
  3. Sparkles: eight-point stars thrown by a press, fading over about a second

Variants

One variant.

States

Drifting (petals rise slowly and sway); twirling (within 220px of the pointer, petals are pulled around it); burst (a press throws 18 sparkles, 28 on a link or button); sleeping (offscreen, no frames are drawn); still (reduced motion: one frame, no pointer response).

Responsive behaviour

The canvas fills the hero at every width. The number of petals follows the area, from 24 to 70, and it redraws on resize at up to 2× device pixels.

Theming

Colours come only from --color-tulle-a to --color-tulle-d, which the canvas reads from CSS. They change with the theme: raspberry, lilac and sunny tints in light, and deeper ones on the plum night.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Decorative: the canvas is aria-hidden and has no role.

Keyboard

None; it never takes focus and never blocks clicks (pointer-events: none).

Contrast

Petals are translucent and sit behind text that keeps its own 4.5:1 contrast on the blush panel.

Motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce it paints one still frame and ignores the pointer.

Content

No text.
