Input group

An input with a leading addon such as the dollar sign.

Overview

tl-input-group joins a fixed addon to the projected input so money fields read as dollars. TuTuMuch trades in US dollars only (L2-018).

Anatomy

  1. AddonSubtle background, aria-hidden
  2. InputProjected .input with inputmode=decimal

Variants

$
$
Price and shipping.

Sizes

Not applicable: one size; follows --control-height-md.

States

$
Enter a price from $1.00 to $5,000.00
Invalid; other states are those of the input.

Responsive behaviour

Fills its column; two groups share a form row from 576px.

Theming

Addon on the subtle background with the strong border.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The input keeps its label; the addon is hidden from assistive technology.

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own.

Focus

Ring on the input.

Labelling

The field label names the input; the currency is in the label's context.

Contrast

Addon text on subtle

Motion

No motion beyond the shared transitions; removed under reduced motion.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

  • Keep the addon to a symbol; put units in the label or help.
