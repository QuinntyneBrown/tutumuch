Closet marquee

Two rows of real listings gliding in opposite directions under the marketing hero, with a live dot and See everything.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.1

Overview

tl-closet-marquee shows the closet within the first scroll of the Phase 1 marketing page: the newest listings from one catalog call. With fewer than 4 listings, or when the call fails, the page leaves it out.

Anatomy

  1. h2 with a raspberry live dot that pulses
  2. Secondary “See everything” button with the arrow-right icon, to /
  3. Two rows, each a list of tiles repeated once for a seamless loop
  4. Tile: the 4:5 photo in a white well, the title on one line and the price in Fraunces

Variants

One variant; the second row scrolls the other way and a little slower (56s against 48s a loop).

States

Gliding; paused (a row stops while it is hovered or holds focus); tile hover (lifts 4px and tips 1°); tile focus (2px focus ring); still (reduced motion).

Responsive behaviour

Tiles are 11rem wide at every width; the rows run edge to edge and clip at the viewport, with no fade mask (no gradients). Under reduced motion each row scrolls sideways by hand.

Theming

The band is the canvas colour; tiles use the photo well and border tokens; the dot is --color-accent. Light and dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A region named by its h2; each row is a list of links. The repeated tiles are aria-hidden with tabindex="-1" and empty alt text, so each listing is announced and reachable once.

Keyboard

Tab moves through the tiles; focus pauses the row so the focused tile stays in place.

Labelling

The link's name is the photo's alt text, the title and the price.

Motion

The rows and the live dot are animations longer than the 250ms transition limit (ADR general/0010); prefers-reduced-motion: reduce stops both and hides the repeats.

Content

Heading of 2 to 4 words, such as “Hand-picked this week”. Titles and prices come from the listings.
