Order summary

The item, its price, shipping, sales tax and total, beside checkout and on order pages.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.5

Overview

tm-order-summary is the .panel titled "Summary": an item row with the thumbnail, then a dl.totals with Item, Shipping, Sales tax and Total. The caller formats the money.

Anatomy

  1. Panel title "Summary"
  2. Item row: thumbnail, title, "brand · size · condition"
  3. Totals: Item, Shipping ("Free" at $0.00), Sales tax, Total with a top rule

Variants

Summary

Air Max 90, whiteNike · Men’s US 12 · Used - Excellent
Item
$85.00
Shipping
$9.00
Sales tax
$8.34
Total
$102.34
Priced: tax for the address and the total the buyer pays.

Sizes

One size. The panel pads --space-6, --space-8 from 768 px.

States

Priced, and not yet priced: before an address is set, Sales tax reads "With your address" and Total reads "—".

Responsive behaviour

In checkout it is the second column of .split from 992 px and follows the form below it.

Theming

Surface background and default border from theme tokens.

Accessibility

An aside labelled by its title, so it is the "Summary" complementary landmark. The amounts are a description list read as term and value. The thumbnail is decorative (alt=""); the title is in the row.

Content

"Summary", "Item", "Shipping", "Free", "Sales tax", "Total". Money always with cents: "$102.34".

Do and don’t

  • Do show the total the buyer will be charged, to the cent (L2-027).
  • Don’t show a total before tax is known.

Tokens

--color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --color-fg-muted, --space-3, --space-4.

Code

<tm-order-summary [item]="item" itemPrice="$85.00" shipping="$9.00" salesTax="$8.34" total="$102.34" />
