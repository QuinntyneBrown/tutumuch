Rating summary

A member's average rating to one decimal place and review count, or No reviews yet.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.9

Overview

tl-rating-summary shows the rating of L2-040 in the profile header. It counts published reviews only. While review authoring is deferred every member has none, so it reads "No reviews yet".

Anatomy

  1. Five stars, the ones above the average rounded to the nearest whole star muted
  2. Average to one decimal place
  3. "({n} reviews)", "review" for one

Variants

4.9(128 reviews)No reviews yet
Rated; no reviews yet.

Sizes

One size, body small.

States

Rated, or none. Not interactive.

Responsive behaviour

Never wraps; the same at every width.

Theming

Stars use the foreground colour; muted stars use the strong border colour in both themes.

Accessibility

The rated variant is one image named "Rated {avg} out of 5 from {n} reviews"; the stars are hidden. "No reviews yet" is plain text.

Content

The average has one decimal place; counts use thousands separators. Never show "0.0".

Do and don't

  • Do show "No reviews yet" rather than empty stars.
  • Don't count hidden or unpublished reviews.

Tokens

--color-fg-default, --color-border-strong, --color-fg-muted, --space-1.

Code

<tl-rating-summary [rating]="profile.rating" />

rating is { average: number | null; count: number }. Source: src/frontend/projects/components/src/lib/rating-summary/.
