Reservation timer

The countdown of the 10 minutes an item is held for the buyer in checkout.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.5

Overview

tl-reservation-timer counts down to the reservation’s expiresAt from TuTuMuch API: "Reserved for you · 9:41 left" (L2-028). It emits its phase so the page can show the warning or danger banner and disable Pay.

Anatomy

  1. Clock icon
  2. "Reserved for you · m:ss left" with tabular figures
  3. A visually hidden polite status for the announcements

Variants

Reserved for you · 9:41 left

Reserved for you · 0:48 left

Reserved for you · 0:00 left

info, warning in the last minute, danger at zero.

Sizes

One size, label type.

States

info more than a minute left; warning in the last minute (.timer--warning, page banner "Less than a minute left to pay. After that, the item goes back on sale."); danger at zero (.timer--danger, banner "Your reservation ended. You weren’t charged." with "Start again", Pay disabled).

Responsive behaviour

Inline under the page title at every width.

Theming

--color-fg-muted, --color-warning-fg and --color-danger-fg per theme.

Accessibility

role="timer" with aria-live="off": the countdown is not read every second. Reaching the last minute and the end are each announced once through a polite status. Colour is never the only signal: the banners carry the words.

Content

"Reserved for you · 9:41 left". Minutes without a leading zero, seconds with one.

Do and don’t

  • Do count from the server’s expiresAt, not from when the page opened.
  • Don’t announce every second.

Tokens

--color-fg-muted, --color-warning-fg, --color-danger-fg, --space-2.

Code

<tl-reservation-timer [expiresAt]="checkout.expiresAt" (phaseChange)="onPhase($event)" />
