Gallery

The photos of one listing: a swipeable main view, dots on small screens and a row of thumbnails.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.4

Overview

tl-gallery shows every photo of a listing in order, cover first. The main view is a horizontal scroll-snap track, so a swipe changes photo below 992 px (L2-063); the thumbnails choose a photo at every width (L2-025).

Anatomy

  1. Main view: one photo at a time, 4:5
  2. Dots, below 992 px only, decorative
  3. Thumbnails: buttons labelled "Show photo {i} of {n}", the shown one with aria-current

Variants

Three photos; one photo shows no dots or thumbnails.

Sizes

Fills its column: the full width below 992 px, the left column from 992 px. sizes="(min-width: 992px) 50vw, 100vw".

States

Selected thumbnail: 2px outline and aria-current="true". The first photo loads eagerly with fetchpriority="high"; the others lazily.

Responsive behaviour

Below 576 px the main view runs edge to edge. Below 992 px dots show and the track is swiped; from 992 px the dots hide.

Theming

Photo background and outline use theme tokens; no theme-specific rules.

Accessibility

A region named "Photos". Thumbnails are buttons of at least 44 by 44 px; Left and Right arrows move between them and show the photo. Each main photo has the alt "Photo {i} of {brand} {title}"; thumbnails have empty alt because the button names them.

Content

No text of its own beyond the button labels.

Do and don't

  • Do keep the cover photo first.
  • Don't autoplay or loop the photos.

Tokens

--color-fg-default, --color-border-strong, --space-2, --space-3.

Code

<tl-gallery [photos]="listing.photos" />
