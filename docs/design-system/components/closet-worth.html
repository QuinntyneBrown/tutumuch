Closet worth

Toggle buttons for outgrown items and a typical total that counts up beside a join call to action.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.1

Overview

tl-closet-worth puts a number on joining: a visitor taps what she has outgrown and sees a typical total on TuTuMuch. The prices are static copy (ADR general/0010).

Anatomy

  1. Eyebrow, h2 (projected as [title], with an accent word in em) and a one-line lede
  2. Chips: pill toggle buttons with the item illustration, the label and the price
  3. Result panel: “Typical total on TuTuMuch”, the total in display type, the item count, [actions] and a [note] line

Variants

One variant. The page decides the call to action: “Join and sell first” for a visitor, and only the note for a member.

States

Chip: off, pressed (raspberry with white text), hover (lifts 2px), focus (2px ring). Total: counting up or down over 450ms with a small bump, or changing at once under reduced motion.

Pressed and off

Responsive behaviour

Stacked below 992px; from 992px the intro and chips take the wider column and the result panel sits beside them.

Theming

The band is blush; the panel is the surface colour; a pressed chip uses --color-accent and --color-fg-on-accent; the total uses the showcase accent. Light and dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A region named by its h2. Each chip is a toggle button with aria-pressed; its name is the label and price (the illustration has empty alt text).

Keyboard

Tab to a chip, Space or Enter toggles it.

Announcements

The counting figure is hidden from assistive technology; a visually hidden polite live region announces the final total, such as “$144 for 4 items”.

Contrast

Pressed chip

Motion

Reduced motion: no count-up, no bump and no chip transitions.

Content

Short item names (“Party dress”, “Winter coat”), whole-dollar typical prices and the label “Typical total on TuTuMuch”. Never promise a sale price.
