Mark

The gingham “T” on a blush circle: the compact logo for the header under 640px, the footer and short waits.

Overview

tl-mark draws the brand kit’s tGingham mark as an inline SVG: a Titan One “T” filled with lilac gingham, turned −6°, with an ink outline and a raspberry shadow, on a blush circle. It is decorative (aria-hidden), never a link on its own, and the only place besides tl-logo where the logo colours and the gingham pattern appear. The favicon and app icon stay the Tilly face tile; the mark is not the app icon.

  • Header under 640px: tl-top-bar shows the mark (36px) instead of the wordmark, inside the same home link, so the page still has one logo link.
  • Footer: the mark, not a second logo link.
  • Short waits: “Checking your link…” and “Confirming your payment” show the mark beside the status line instead of a spinner.

Anatomy

  1. CircleBlush, --color-mark-bg, the full box
  2. LetterTitan One “T”, 0.76 × size, turned −6°
  3. Gingham fillCells 0.05 × size in --color-logo-gingham-light and -dark
  4. ShadowRaspberry copy offset 0.07 / 0.09 × letter, --color-logo-shadow
  5. OutlineInk stroke 0.1 × letter, round joins, --color-logo-outline

Variants

One variant. The kit’s other marks (tuBlush, ginghamTile, raspberryT) are not built (ADR general/0009).

Sizes

16px (the minimum), 24px (.mark--sm), 36px (default, --size-illustration-sm), 64px.

Input size is in pixels, default 36, minimum 16; smaller values are clamped to 16. Keep clear space of at least the height of the “u” of the wordmark (about a quarter of the mark) on every side.

States

The mark has no interactive states. Inside the header’s home link it takes the link’s focus ring; on a short wait it sits beside a role="status" line while the container carries aria-busy="true".

Checking your link…
Short wait, from pages/verify-email/loading. No spinner for page-level waits.

Responsive behaviour

The mark does not scale with the viewport; the size is set by its context. Under 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm) the header hides the wordmark and shows the mark; from 640px the wordmark returns. Classes: .logo--responsive with .logo__wordmark and .logo__mark.

The responsive home link: wordmark from 640px, mark below. Narrow the window to see the swap.

Theming

The mark keeps its colours in both themes: --color-mark-bg stays blush on the plum night, like the logo art. Only tl-logo and tl-mark may use the --color-logo-* tokens and the gingham pattern.

Both themes

Accessibility

Role

Decorative: aria-hidden="true" and focusable="false". In the header the surrounding link is named “TuTuMuch home”; on waits the status text says what is happening.

Contrast

The mark is not text and carries no meaning, so no contrast minimum applies; the ink outline keeps the “T” visible on every allowed background.

Motion

None. Never animate the mark letter by letter or spin it as a loader.

Content

No text. Never place words inside the circle, and never pair the mark with a retyped “TuTuMuch” to fake the wordmark.

Do and don't

  • Do keep one logo link per page: in the header the mark replaces the wordmark inside the same link.
  • Don’t put the mark on a photo, a pattern or lilac; use white, cream, blush or raspberry.
  • Don’t use the mark as a favicon; the app icon is the Tilly face tile.
