Video poster

The video’s poster frame as one large button that opens the video dialog. Nothing plays until it is pressed.

Overview

tl-video-poster shows the pitch video’s last slide in the hero of /welcome. Pressing it opens the pitch video dialog.

Anatomy

  1. Button filling a 16:9 frame with soft corners
  2. Poster image (decorative, alt="")
  3. Play label “Play” on a cream pill
  4. Length tag “0:30”

Variants

One variant.

Default

Sizes

One size; it fills its column.

States

Default, hover (label softens), focus (cream outline), active. The video never autoplays.

Hover and focus

Responsive behaviour

Fills its column at 16:9.

Theming

Plum frame in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native button named by its visible label.

Keyboard

Enter and Space open the dialog through DialogService.

Focus

Focus returns here when the dialog closes.

Labelling

The visible label names the button; the image is decorative.

Contrast

Label on the play pill

Motion

None; nothing plays until pressed.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

Say the length in the label.
