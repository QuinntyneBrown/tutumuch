Buy bar

The purchase actions of a listing, fixed to the bottom of the screen on phones and tablets.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.4

Overview

tl-buy-bar holds the total with "incl. shipping" and the projected actions: "Buy now", or "Edit listing" for the seller. A Reserved or Sold listing has no buy bar.

Anatomy

  1. Total and caption (hidden from 992 px, and when there are two actions)
  2. Projected actions, large buttons

Variants

$94.00incl. shipping
Buy now
Buy now for a member or a visitor; the seller sees Edit listing.

Sizes

One size; buttons use --control-height-lg.

States

Static. A visitor's Buy now links to sign in with the listing as the return URL (L2-026).

Responsive behaviour

Below 992 px: position: sticky; bottom: 0 with safe-area padding, full bleed, so Buy now stays on screen while scrolling (L2-063). From 992 px: inline in the info column without the total.

Theming

Surface background and top border from theme tokens.

Accessibility

Actions are links or buttons of at least 44 px high. The bar is not a landmark; its order in the DOM follows the price.

Content

"Buy now", "Edit listing", "incl. shipping". Money with cents: "$94.00".

Do and don't

  • Do show the total including shipping.
  • Don't show Buy now to the seller or on a Reserved or Sold listing.

Tokens

--color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --z-sticky, --layout-margin.

Code

<tl-buy-bar [total]="'$94.00'">
  <a tlButton="primary" size="lg" routerLink="/checkout/L-10001">Buy now</a>
</tl-buy-bar>
