Seller summary

Who sells the item: avatar, display name linking to the profile, sales and rating, or New seller.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.4

Overview

tl-seller-summary closes the listing info column. A seller with no completed sales shows the badge "New seller" in place of the sales count and rating (L2-025).

Anatomy

  1. Avatar with initials
  2. Display name, a link to /members/{displayName}
  3. "{n} sales"
  4. Rating: stars, average and "({n} reviews)", or the "New seller" badge

Variants

KI
kicks_12141 sales4.9(128 reviews)
JA
jaybirdNew seller
Established seller; new seller.

Sizes

One size.

States

Established or new. Until orders and reviews record them, every seller is a new seller.

Responsive behaviour

The same at every width.

Theming

Avatar and badge use accent and neutral tokens.

Accessibility

The avatar is hidden from assistive technology. The rating is one image named "Rated {avg} out of 5 from {n} reviews".

Content

Numbers use thousands separators; the average has one decimal place.

Do and don't

  • Do show "New seller" rather than an empty rating.
  • Don't show the seller's email, real name or address (L2-054).

Tokens

--color-accent-subtle, --color-fg-accent, --color-border-default, --space-4.

Code

<tl-seller-summary [seller]="listing.seller" />
