Alert

An inline message with an icon, a title and one line of text, placed above the form or content it is about.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.2

Overview

Alerts report the outcome of an action on the page itself: a refused sign-in, a saved draft, a sent link. tl-alert picks the icon from the tone. Danger alerts are announced at once (role="alert"); pass live="polite" for a status message such as success.

Anatomy

  1. IconFrom the tone; aria-hidden
  2. Title--text-label, says what happened
  3. TextWhat to do next; may hold a reference such as Ref 5c1d-0e7a

Variants

Info
Neutral information.
Draft saved
Finish it any time from your listings.
Too many requests
You can request 3 links an hour. Try again later.
Couldn’t create your account
Try again in a moment. Ref 0c4e-7b21
info, success, warning, danger.

Sizes

Not applicable: one size; alerts fill the width of their container.

States

Link sent
Earlier links no longer work.
Something went wrong
Try again in a moment. Ref 5d1c-0a77
Static once shown; success is polite, danger is assertive.

Responsive behaviour

Alerts fill their column at every width; text wraps under the title.

Theming

Each tone uses its subtle background, border and foreground tokens; the icon takes the tone's foreground.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="alert" for danger (default), role="status" with live="polite", none for info and warning.

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own.

Focus

Not focusable; links inside take focus in order.

Labelling

The title is the message; never rely on colour alone.

Contrast

Danger text on danger subtle
Success text on success subtle

Motion

No motion beyond the shared transitions; removed under reduced motion.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

  • Title says what happened: “Draft saved”, “Email or password is incorrect”.
  • Text says what to do next. Server failures add the reference: “Ref 5c1d-0e7a”.

Do and don't

Tokens

Code

<tl-alert tone="success" heading="Draft saved" live="polite">
  Finish it any time from <a routerLink="/account/listings">your listings</a>.
</tl-alert>

Project links or buttons with the actions attribute; they sit after the text, inline in a banner. dismissible adds a “Dismiss” close button and emits (dismissed); the owner hides the alert. The payout banner uses both: <tl-alert tone="info" banner live="polite" dismissible (dismissed)="dismiss()">$33.30 is on its way to Chase •••• 6021. <a actions routerLink="/account/payouts">Payouts</a></tl-alert>.

Input or memberEffect
toneinfo | success | warning | danger
headingTitle text (required)
iconOverrides the tone icon, for example clock
liveassertive | polite | off; danger defaults to assertive
