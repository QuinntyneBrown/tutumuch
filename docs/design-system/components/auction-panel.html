Auction panel

Shows an auction’s current or starting bid, bid count, time left, end time and the viewer’s own standing.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.5

Overview

tl-auction-panel takes the price block’s place on an auction’s detail page (L2-114). It reads the auction object of the listing response (currentPrice, startingBid, bidCount, endsAt, ended, viewerIsHighestBidder, viewerHasBid, viewerIsWinner, payBy) and shows only what the viewer may know: never a bidder’s name or anyone else’s bid (L2-120). The actions, “Place bid” and “Pay now”, stay in the Buy bar below it.

Anatomy

  1. Label“Current bid”, “Starting bid” before any bid, “Winning bid” once ended
  2. AmountPrice type, large, tabular figures
  3. Bid count“No bids yet”, “1 bid”, “3 bids”
  4. Time left“Ends in 2d 4h”; “Ends in 42 min” under an hour; absent once ended
  5. End date and timeA time element in the viewer’s time zone, with the shipping cost
  6. Status lineOptional: the viewer’s own standing, in a tone

Variants

The status line has four tones: info for the highest bidder, warning for an outbid member, success for the winner and neutral (subtle surface, ink text, clock icon) for an ended auction or the seller.

Sizes

One size; it fills the info column like the price block it replaces.

States

Running, ending soon (under an hour), ended. The time left is recomputed each minute from endsAt; when it reaches zero the owner reloads the listing, so the panel moves to the ended variant without a bid being possible.

Ending soon: under an hour the time left reads in minutes, in the warning colour.

Responsive behaviour

Full width of the info column at every width. Below 992 px the sticky buy bar repeats the amount with its label (“Current bid”, “Starting bid”, “Winning bid”) next to “Place bid” or “Pay now”; the facts line wraps between the bid count and the time left.

Theming

No surface of its own; the status line uses the info, warning and success alert pairs (--color-*-bg with --color-*-fg) and the neutral line --color-bg-subtle with --color-fg-default, so both themes come from the tokens. No new colours.

Accessibility

  • A section with aria-label="Auction".
  • The time left is plain text, not a live region: it updates each minute without being announced. The end date and time are a time element with a machine-readable datetime.
  • The status line has role="status", so a change of standing (outbid, won, ended) is announced politely; its tone is never the only signal: the icon and the words carry it.
  • Text meets 4.5:1 on its tone in both themes; the ending-soon warning text meets 4.5:1 on the canvas.

Content

Labels: “Current bid”, “Starting bid”, “Winning bid”. Counts: “No bids yet”, “1 bid”, “{n} bids”. Time: “Ends in 2d 4h”, “Ends in 42 min”. Status: “You’re the highest bidder”, “You’ve been outbid”, “You won”, “This auction has ended”. Never “Buy now” on an auction, and never a bidder’s name.

Do and don't

  • Do put “Place bid” and “Pay now” in the buy bar, not inside the panel.
  • Do show the end time in the viewer’s time zone.
  • Don't show a bid history, a bidder count by name or another member’s bid.
  • Don't use it for a fixed-price listing: that keeps the price block.

Tokens

--space-1, --space-2, --space-3, --space-4, --text-label, --text-h2, --text-body-sm, --font-weight-semibold, --radius-input, --color-fg-default, --color-fg-muted, --color-bg-subtle, --color-info-bg, --color-info-fg, --color-warning-bg, --color-warning-fg, --color-success-bg, --color-success-fg.
