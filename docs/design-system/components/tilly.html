Tilly

Tilly is TuTuMuch’s mascot: a joyful Black girl, about six, with two afro puffs tied with raspberry bows. One per screen, in an approved pose.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNew in v0.4

Overview

tl-tilly places one of Tilly’s poses and writes its alt text. She represents the community, so she is used with care: in the marketing hero, on sport category headers, in empty states and on the new-listing confirmation. She speaks to the girl; the copy around her speaks to the parent.

  • At most one Tilly per screen. A page that already shows her in its header uses a plain empty state.
  • Never crop off her puffs, recolour her skin or hair, flip her or stretch her.
  • Never put her next to product photos in a way that makes her look like a model wearing the item.
  • Don’t generate new versions of Tilly. The artwork comes from docs/print/characters/.

Anatomy

  1. ArtworkCut-out WebP, 600px tall, transparent background
  2. Alt textNames Tilly and what she is doing; written by the component
  3. BoxHeight from the size; width follows the art’s ratio

Alt-text rule

The alt text names her and what she is doing, for example alt="Tilly kicking a soccer ball". tl-tilly writes these exact strings, so a screen never ships a Tilly without alt text and never uses alt="" for her. Don’t add a caption that repeats the alt text.

States

Tilly is an image with no interactive states. She is never a button or a link, and she does not change on hover or focus.

Dance

Girls
Category header (.category-head): the title, the size tag and the matching pose on a blush panel.

Responsive behaviour

  • The header pose is 8rem tall below 768px and --size-illustration-lg from 768px; the panel keeps the title on the left.
  • In the marketing hero she sits above the video poster in one column below 992px and beside the copy from 992px.
  • She keeps her ratio at every width (height set, width: auto, max-width: 100%).

Theming

The artwork is the same in both themes and never recoloured. On the plum night she sits on the blush panel or the dark canvas; her own colours do not change.

Both themes

Accessibility

Text alternative

Every Tilly has alt text that names her and the action (WCAG 1.1.1). The pose table above lists the exact strings.

Count

At most one per screen, which Playwright checks on every routed page (L2-096).

Motion

None. Tilly does not bounce, wiggle or fade in on load.

Content

  • Speak to the parent in the copy; let Tilly speak to the girl.
  • Her catchphrase is “Never too much tutu!”. Use it once at most, in marketing moments only. It is the one exclamation point a page may have.
  • Empty states with Tilly have one short line and one action: “The closet is empty for now” → Sell outgrown gear.

Do and don't

  • Don’t place Tilly beside a listing photo as if she were wearing the item.
  • Don’t crop her puffs or stretch her to fill a box.
  • Don’t use Tilly as wallpaper, a pattern or a loading spinner.
