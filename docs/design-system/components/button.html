Button

Buttons trigger actions: a raspberry primary that hovers to ink, a ringed secondary, ghost, link and danger. One primary per view.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

A button performs an action; a link goes somewhere. Primary (tlButton="primary") is a raspberry pill with a white semibold label that hovers to ink. Secondary (tlButton, the default) is a white pill with a raspberry label and a 2px inset raspberry ring that fills raspberry with a white label on hover, over 150ms. Ghost is text with a quiet hover fill, link is semibold raspberry that turns ink and underlines (4px offset) on hover, and danger is for destructive confirmations. There is no lip and no lift. Only one primary button per view.

Anatomy

  1. Leading icon (optional)1.25em, aria-hidden
  2. LabelA verb, sentence case, 16px semibold
  3. ContainerPill, --radius-full, --control-height-*; secondary adds a 2px inset ring

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px the primary action of a form or page header becomes .btn--block.
  • Dialog and sheet footers keep buttons on one row and wrap when needed; the primary stays last.
  • Labels never wrap to two lines at desktop width.

Theming

Component tokens (--btn-bg, --btn-fg, --btn-ring, --btn-bg-hover, --btn-fg-hover) alias semantic tokens per variant. In dark the accent becomes raspberry 400 with a plum label, and primary hover lightens instead of turning ink.

Accessibility

Role

Native <button>; a link styled as a button stays an <a href>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocus in DOM order.
Enter, SpaceActivate.

Focus

Every button shows a 2px raspberry ring with a 2px offset (--shadow-focus), which tlButton provides.

Labelling

The label is the name; icon-only buttons set aria-label. Busy adds aria-busy and keeps the name.

Contrast

Label on danger
Focus ring on surface

White on raspberry is used only at 16px semibold or larger.

Motion

Background, colour and ring transition over --duration-fast (150ms) with --ease-out, named properties only; no movement. Zero under reduced motion.

Touch

Medium and large are 44px and 48px; small (32px) appears only inside rows that are targets.

Content

  • Buttons are verbs, in sentence case: “Shop the closet”, “Sell outgrown gear”, “Buy now”, “Save changes”. Never OK, Submit or Click here.
  • No exclamation points in labels.
  • Destructive labels name the loss: “Close account”.
  • Three words or fewer where possible.
