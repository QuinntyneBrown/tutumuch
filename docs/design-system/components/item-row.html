Item row

One item on one line: an optional thumbnail, the title and a muted meta line.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.2

Overview

tl-item-row summarises an item in panels and lists: the sell success panel, orders and payouts. The title truncates; the meta line carries brand, size and price joined by middle dots.

Anatomy

  1. ThumbnailProjected [thumb], optional
  2. Title--text-label, one line
  3. Meta--text-body-sm, muted

Variants

Floral party dressMini Boden · Girls’ · $42.00
In a panel, without a thumbnail until photos exist.

Sizes

Not applicable: one size; the thumbnail decides the height when present.

States

A very long title that keeps going until it has to be cut short at the end of the rowBrand · Girls’ · $45.00
Long titles truncate with an ellipsis.

Responsive behaviour

Fills its container; the title truncates rather than wraps.

Theming

Default and muted foregrounds.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain text; wrap it in a link when the row is a target.

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own.

Focus

Not focusable on its own.

Labelling

The title names the item.

Contrast

Meta on surface

Motion

No motion beyond the shared transitions; removed under reduced motion.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

  • Meta order: brand · size · price.
