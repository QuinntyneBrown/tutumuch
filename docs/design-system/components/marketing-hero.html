Marketing hero

The plum showcase band that opens and closes the marketing page: eyebrow, a 3 to 6 word headline, one supporting line, two calls to action and optional media.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

tl-marketing-hero is the plum showcase band that opens and closes the marketing page at /welcome. It says what TuTuMuch is in a few words: everything here is for girls.

Anatomy

  1. Showcase surface: --gradient-showcase, plum in both themes
  2. Decorative tl-shapes behind the content: party-pop blobs and a polka-dot band
  3. Eyebrow in overline type
  4. Headline (h1 in the hero, h2 in the closing band) with a pink em accent
  5. Lede: one line
  6. Actions: a primary and a secondary pill button
  7. Media slot (the video poster) in the second column from 992px

Variants

hero (two columns with media, h1) and closing (one column, h2, quiet shapes).

Sizes

One size; it fills its container.

States

Static. Buttons keep their hover, active and focus states, re-pointed to the showcase tokens: a primary button is cream on plum and the focus ring is cream.

Responsive behaviour

One column below 992px with the media after the actions; two equal columns from 992px. Padding --space-16/--space-20, --space-24 from 992px. The headline scales with clamp().

Theming

The showcase stays plum in light and dark, like the logo’s outline, so its contrast never changes: cream text 15.3:1 on the dark end and 9.4:1 on the light end; the pink emphasis 7.8:1 and 4.8:1 (display size only).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section labelled by its heading. The headline is the page h1 (hero) or an h2 (closing).

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own; the actions are native links.

Focus

Focus rings are cream on plum.

Labelling

headingId labels the section.

Contrast

Cream text on the dark end
Cream text on the light end
Lede on the dark end
Lede on the light end
Pink emphasis on the dark end
Pink emphasis on the light end (display only)
Primary button label on the showcase

Motion

The shapes drift slowly and stop under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

  • Headline 3 to 6 words, one supporting sentence, two actions at most.
  • Say that everything is for girls; never promise boys’ or adult sizes (ADR general/0005).
