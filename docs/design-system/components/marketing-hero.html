Marketing hero

The solid blush panel that opens and closes the marketing page: a short headline, one supporting line, one primary action, and Tilly twirling above the video poster.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

tl-marketing-hero opens the marketing page at /welcome and closes it. It is a solid blush panel: no gradient, no decorative shapes. The opening hero carries the page’s only primary button (“Shop the closet”) and, in its media column, Tilly’s twirl pose above the video poster; the closing band uses secondary buttons only and no Tilly, so the page keeps one Tilly and one primary.

Anatomy

  1. Panel: --color-showcase-start, a solid blush (plum 850 in dark)
  2. Label: a short sentence-case line in --text-label, no dot, no capitals
  3. Headline: --text-display (Fraunces 600), h1 in the hero and h2 in the closing band, with a raspberry em
  4. Lede: one line
  5. Actions: one primary and one secondary in the hero; secondary only in the closing band
  6. Media (hero only): Tilly twirling (tl-tilly pose="hero") above tl-video-poster

Variants

hero (two columns with media, h1) and closing (one column, h2, no media).

Sizes

One size; it fills its container. Tilly is --size-illustration-xl tall.

States

Static. Buttons keep their own hover, active and focus states; the focus ring offset takes the panel colour.

Responsive behaviour

One column below 992px with the media after the actions; two equal columns from 992px. Padding --space-16/--space-20, --space-24 from 992px. The headline scales with clamp() up to 64px.

Theming

Light: ink text on blush. Dark: plum-50 text on a plum 850 panel, with a raspberry 300 emphasis. Tilly and the poster never change.

Accessibility

Role

A section labelled by its heading (headingId). The headline is the page h1 in the hero and an h2 in the closing band. Tilly has her alt text; the poster is a single button named by its label.

Contrast

Headline on the panel
Lede on the panel
Emphasis on the panel

Motion

None: no drifting shapes and no entrance animation.

Touch

Large buttons are 48px.

Content

  • Headline 3 to 6 words, one supporting sentence.
  • One primary per view: the hero’s “Shop the closet”. The closing band and the feature bands use secondary buttons.
  • Say that everything is for girls; never promise boys’ or adult sizes (ADR general/0005).
  • Tilly’s catchphrase, if used, appears once on the page.
