Spinner

An in-place busy indicator inside a control: a submit button, Load more, or video buffering. Never for a page load.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.2

Overview

tl-spinner shows that a control is busy in place: a button that is submitting, Load more fetching the next page, or a video buffering. Page loads use Skeleton blocks shaped like the content, and short page-level waits (“Checking your link…”, “Confirming your payment”) show the mark with a status line. The spinner is hidden from assistive technology; the control carries aria-busy="true".

Anatomy

  1. SpinnerAnimated ring, aria-hidden
  2. MessageMuted text, role=status

Variants

Inside a primary button, inside Load more, and the bare ring used by the video player.

Sizes

Not applicable: one size; the large spinner modifier arrives with the screen that needs it.

States

Running: the label is hidden, the name stays, clicks are ignored; replaced by the result.

Responsive behaviour

Inline cluster at every width.

Theming

A raspberry arc (--color-accent) over the thin ink/10 ring (--color-border-default); inside a primary button it takes the label colour.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Message in role="status".

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The message says what is happening.

Contrast

Message on canvas

Motion

No motion beyond the shared transitions; removed under reduced motion.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

The control keeps its verb (“Publish listing”, “Load more”); a status line says what is happening when the wait is longer than a moment.

Do and don't

Tokens
