Provider button

Continue with a third-party account such as Google, above an “or” divider that leads into the email form. The provider’s mark stays in its own colours; everything else is the secondary pill.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.1

Overview

tl-provider-button is a link styled as the secondary Button (lg, block) that carries the provider’s mark and the label “Continue with Google”. It sits first on /sign-in and /join, above tl-or-divider and the email form, and only renders when TuTuMuch has a client for that provider (L2-088). It is a link, not a button: pressing it leaves the page for the provider through /api/auth/google/start; no provider script ever runs in the page.

Anatomy

  1. PillSecondary button, lg, full width
  2. MarkThe provider’s mark at 1.25rem, decorative, in the provider’s own colours
  3. Label“Continue with Google”, bold
  4. DividerTwo thin ink/10 lines around a small sentence-case “or”

Variants

One provider today: google. The mark is the “G” Google publishes for sign-in buttons and is used as published (Google’s sign-in branding guidelines): never recoloured, stretched, cropped or redrawn, in both themes.

Sizes

One size: lg and block, the same as the form’s submit button beneath it, so the two ways in weigh the same.

States

Default, hover, focus, active, busy (the mark hides under the spinner and the name stays) and disabled. Busy lasts only while the browser leaves for the provider.

Responsive behaviour

Fills the auth card at every width (under 576px to over 1200px). The label never wraps at the card’s minimum width.

Theming

Surface, text and border follow the secondary button’s tokens in both themes. The mark is the one element outside the tokens: Google’s four brand colours are fixed by Google and are the same in light and dark, which Giddy allows for a third party’s mark (ADR accounts/0001).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <a href> to the start endpoint; the divider is role="separator" with aria-label="or".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFirst control of the card, before the form.
EnterLeaves for the provider.

Focus

Shared ring. After the provider sends the person back, focus starts at the top of the sign-in page with the result alert, if any, first in reading order.

Labelling

The visible label names the action and the provider; the mark is aria-hidden. Busy keeps the accessible name.

Contrast

Motion

The button’s shared transitions; nothing else moves.

Touch

Full width and --control-height-lg tall.

Content

“Continue with Google” on both the sign-in and the join page, because the same press signs a member in or starts a join. Google’s approved labels are “Sign in with Google”, “Sign up with Google” and “Continue with Google”; use no other wording. The divider says “or”, lower case.
