Shapes

Decorative party-pop blobs and a polka-dot band in raspberry, lilac and sunny that sit behind the showcase.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

tl-shapes adds Giddy’s playful party pops to the showcase: a soft raspberry blob, a lilac circle, a sunny dot and a polka-dot band along the bottom edge. Used inside tl-marketing-hero on /welcome.

Anatomy

  1. Brand blob: raspberry, an organic rounded shape, 45% opacity below 992px
  2. Tone circle: lilac
  3. Light dot: sunny, from 992px only
  4. Polka-dot band: raspberry dots along the bottom edge

Variants

full (hero) and quiet (tone circle and dots only, for the closing band).

Full
Quiet

Sizes

One size; it fills its container. Shape sizes follow the viewport with min().

States

The shapes drift slowly (--duration-drift, 24 s, alternate). Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the animation is removed.

Responsive behaviour

The brand blob is faint below 992px, where text can sit over it, and full strength from 992px, where it sits beside the text. The sunny dot shows from 992px only.

Theming

Theme-independent: drawn on the plum showcase only.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Purely decorative: aria-hidden="true", pointer-events: none, behind content.

Keyboard

None.

Focus

Never focusable.

Labelling

None; shapes carry no meaning.

Contrast

Shapes are decoration and are not text backgrounds: keep body text off the full-strength blob, circle and dot (cream on lilac is only 3.1:1).

Motion

Reduced motion stops all movement (WCAG 2.2.2, 2.3.3).

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

No text, icons or meaning in shapes.
