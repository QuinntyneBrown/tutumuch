Video player

A captioned video with native controls plus Play/Pause, Captions and a transcript link, and clear loading and error states.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

tl-video-player plays the pitch video inside the “TuTuMuch in 30 seconds” dialog.

Anatomy

  1. 16:9 plum frame with the video (MP4 then WebM sources, English captions track)
  2. State overlay: spinner while loading, message and Try again on error
  3. Controls: Play/Pause, Captions (toggle), Read the transcript

Variants

One variant, used inside the pitch video dialog.

Read the transcript
Paused

Sizes

One size; it fills its container.

States

Loading (role=status spinner), playing (Pause), paused (Play), captions on (aria-pressed=true, track showing), error (role=alert, Try again; the transcript link stays).

Responsive behaviour

Fills its container at 16:9; controls wrap.

Theming

The frame is plum in both themes; controls use page tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Never autoplays on load: playback starts only from a press. Captions are WebVTT; a plain-text transcript is always linked (WCAG 1.2.1, 1.2.2).

Keyboard

Space toggles while the video has focus; Play/Pause and Captions are native buttons.

Focus

Focus stays in the dialog; Escape closes it and stops the video.

Labelling

Captions uses aria-pressed.

Contrast

Error text in the frame
Error hint in the frame

Motion

None of its own.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

Error copy says what happened and what to do; no codes. “The video didn’t load”.
