Steps

Three numbered steps with a short title and one line each, on a raspberry top rule.

Overview

tl-steps shows how selling on TuTuMuch works, inside the How it works band of /welcome.

Anatomy

  1. Raspberry top rule
  2. Two-digit number from a CSS counter
  3. Title h3
  4. One muted line

Variants

One variant.

  1. List it

    Add photos, a price and the condition.

  2. Sell it

    A buyer pays. You ship within 5 business days.

  3. Get paid

    The order completes and your payout is on its way.

List it, Sell it, Get paid

Sizes

One size; it fills its container.

States

Static.

Responsive behaviour

Stacked below 768px, three columns from 768px.

Theming

Semantic tokens; light and dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An ordered list, so screen readers announce the count and order; titles are h3 under the band’s h2.

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The band’s h2 names the list.

Contrast

Step text

Motion

None.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

Two words per title, one line of text.
