Feature band

A generous page band with a short label, a headline, an optional secondary action and up to three points, on blush or the surface.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

tl-feature-band carries the For shoppers, For sellers and How it works bands of /welcome. Bands are separated by a background change (blush, surface, canvas) and spacing, not borders. Their actions are secondary buttons: the page’s one primary lives in the hero.

Anatomy

  1. Label: a short sentence-case line (“For shoppers”), muted, no dot
  2. Headline: h2, 3 to 6 words, Fraunces
  3. Optional action: one secondary button
  4. Points (bold title and one muted line each, thin ink/10 line above) or a projected body such as Steps

Variants

blush (a solid blush band), surface (white), default (the cream canvas), and stacked (body below the heading, for steps).

Sizes

One size; it fills its container.

States

Static.

Responsive behaviour

Heading and body stack below 992px; 5:7 columns from 992px. Padding --space-16, --space-24 from 768px.

Theming

Semantic tokens; on blush the point text uses --color-fg-subtle so it keeps 4.5:1. In dark the blush band becomes a deep raspberry.

Accessibility

Role

A section labelled by its h2; points are a list.

Contrast

Point text on blush
Point text on the surface
Label on canvas

Motion

None; bands do not animate in on scroll.

Content

Three points at most, each a few words plus one short line. Say what is for girls; never name boys’ or adult sizes. Labels are sentence case, never letterspaced capitals.
