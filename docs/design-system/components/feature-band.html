Feature band

A generous page band with an eyebrow, a short headline, an optional action and either up to three points or a projected body such as steps.

Overview

tl-feature-band carries the For shoppers, For sellers and How it works bands of the marketing page at /welcome.

Anatomy

  1. Eyebrow with a small raspberry dot
  2. Headline h2, 3 to 6 words
  3. Optional actions
  4. Points (bold title and one muted line each) or a projected body

Variants

default on the cream canvas, surface on the card surface (alternating bands), stacked (body below the heading, for steps).

For shoppers

Everything here is for girls.

Browse the catalog
  • Every listing is for girlsClothes, dance and sports gear, cameras and more. Nothing to filter out.
  • Outgrown, not worn outMost things still have plenty of twirl left in them.
  • Honest conditionFive grades, and flaws named up front.
Default

For sellers

Pass it on to the next girl.

Start selling
  • List in minutesPhotos, a price and the condition. That’s it.
  • Families are already lookingSell to parents who need exactly what she has outgrown.
Surface

How it works

Three steps. That’s it.

  1. List it

    Add photos, a price and the condition.

  2. Sell it

    A buyer pays. You ship within 5 business days.

  3. Get paid

    The order completes and your payout is on its way.

Stacked

Sizes

One size; it fills its container.

States

Static.

Responsive behaviour

Heading and body stack below 992px; 5:7 columns from 992px. Padding --space-16, --space-24 from 768px.

Theming

Semantic page tokens; works unchanged in light and dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section labelled by its h2; points are a list; the dot is a pseudo-element with no content.

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own.

Focus

Actions use the button focus ring.

Labelling

headingId labels the section.

Contrast

Point text
Point text on the surface

Motion

None.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

Three points at most, each a few words plus one short line. Say what is for girls; never name boys’ or adult sizes.
