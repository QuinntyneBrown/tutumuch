Size tag

The Tutu Much size mark, “US 12” or “L”, with the number emphasised.

Overview

tm-size-tag is the visual signature of Tutu Much: every listing is men's US 12 or men's L. It appears on listing photos and in the fixed size of the sell form.

Anatomy

  1. PrefixUS for shoes
  2. ValueBold number or letter

Variants

US 12 L
Shoes and clothing.

Sizes

US 12 US 12
Default and large.

States

L
Static.

Responsive behaviour

Fixed size; never wraps.

Theming

Ink on paper in light, paper on ink in dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Text with aria-label="Size US 12".

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The aria-label reads the whole size.

Contrast

Tag text on canvas

Motion

No motion beyond the shared transitions; removed under reduced motion.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

  • Only “US 12” and “L” exist.
