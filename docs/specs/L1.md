# L1 - High-Level Requirements

TuTuMuch is an open marketplace for all things young girls: new and gently loved clothes, shoes, dance gear, sports equipment, cameras and tech, toys and crafts, every item for girls. Parents and families join to sell what their daughters have outgrown and to buy what comes next. It is a full-stack, responsive web application with an Angular frontend and a .NET (ASP.NET Core) backend.

Each L1 below is refined into testable L2 requirements in [L2.md](L2.md).

## L1-001: Open membership
Anyone aged 18 or over shall be able to create a TuTuMuch account, verify it, manage their profile and close it. Every verified member shall be able to both buy and sell, with no seller approval step.

## L1-002: Authentication and access control
The system shall authenticate members securely, manage their sessions, let them recover access to their account, and allow each member to act only on the data and actions that belong to them.

## L1-003: Size-locked catalog
The system shall accept only items for girls, in the categories TuTuMuch supports (clothing, shoes, dance, sports, cameras and tech, toys and crafts); every listing carries the one accepted size, Girls' (an audience lock, ADR general/0006). The backend shall enforce this rule for every listing, whatever the client sends.

## L1-004: Listing management
Sellers shall be able to create, photograph, describe, price, publish, edit and withdraw listings for new and used items, and see all of their listings in one place.

## L1-005: Catalog browsing and search
Anyone, signed in or not, shall be able to browse the catalog of active listings and narrow it by keyword, category, brand, condition and price.

## L1-006: Listing detail
Anyone shall be able to view a single listing with everything needed to decide whether to buy it, including its photos, condition, price, shipping cost and seller reputation.

## L1-007: Purchasing and payment
Verified members shall be able to buy a listed item, pay for it securely through a third-party payment provider, and be guaranteed that each item is sold exactly once. Sellers shall be paid once the sale is complete.

## L1-008: Order fulfilment
The system shall track every order from payment through shipping and delivery to completion, and shall handle cancellations, refunds and problems with an order.

## L1-009: Ratings and reviews
Each member shall have a public reputation made of a rating summary of published reviews. Writing reviews after a sale is deferred; see [ADR 0001](../adr/general/0001-defer-moderation-reviews-and-data-export.md).

## L1-010: Member profiles
Each member shall have a public profile that shows their listings, ratings and reviews without exposing private contact or address details.

## L1-011: Notifications
The system shall tell members, by email, about events that affect their account, their listings and their orders.

## L1-013: Security
The system shall protect members, their data and their money against the OWASP Top 10 and other common web application threats.

## L1-014: Privacy and data protection
The system shall collect only the personal data it needs, keep it private, protect it at rest and in transit, and let members erase their data.

## L1-015: Performance and scalability
The system shall respond quickly on phones and desktops and shall keep doing so as the number of members and listings grows.

## L1-016: Responsive design
Every page and dialog shall work on extra small (<576px), small (>=576px), medium (>=768px), large (>=992px) and extra large (>=1200px) viewports.

## L1-017: Accessibility
The web application shall be usable by people with disabilities and shall conform to WCAG 2.2 level AA.

## L1-018: Reliability and observability
The system shall be highly available, recover from failure without losing data, report errors consistently, and give operators the logs, metrics and alerts they need to run it.

## L1-019: Developer workspace
A developer shall be able to clone the repository and, with one command, restore, build, lint and test the backend solution and the frontend workspace on Windows and on Linux. A developer shall also be able to reset a local install and load demo data with one command.

## L1-020: Marketing page
The web application shall have a public marketing page that explains TuTuMuch to shoppers and sellers in short copy and a short captioned pitch video (under 30 seconds), shows a preview image when its link is shared, and leads each audience to the catalog or to selling. It shall say that everything on TuTuMuch is for girls (clothes, dance and sports gear, cameras and more) and shall never promise boys' or adult sizes.

## L1-021: Launch phases
The system shall open in three launch phases chosen by one configuration setting, with no code change or data migration to move between them. In Phase 1 (the default) only the owner, signed in as an administrator, may list items, the catalog is one flat list without category menus, and it holds fewer than 40 listings. In Phase 2 the owner is still the only seller and every category menu is shown. In Phase 3 every verified member may sell. Buying, accounts and orders work the same in every phase, and the web application never offers selling to someone the current phase does not allow to sell.

## L1-022: Page guidelines
Every page, dialog and notification of the web application shall follow the TuTuMuch page guidelines (`docs/page-guidelines.md`): the brand assets (wordmark, mark, Tilly), the palette, the type roles, the voice and standard terms, the page skeleton and layout, the marketplace patterns, designed loading, empty, error, success and sold-out states, quick motion that respects reduced motion, imagery with meaningful alt text, and accessibility. The mocks, the design system and the implemented pages shall agree with them.

## L1-023: Item requests
A signed-in member shall be able to ask TuTuMuch for an item that is not in the closet yet, by category, keywords and, optionally, a highest price and a lowest condition, under the size rule of `L1-003`. When a listing that matches an open request becomes active, the member shall be told by email (`L1-011`) and shall see the match with their requests. Members shall be able to view, edit and delete their own requests and turn the emails off; requests are private to their owner (`L1-002`, `L1-014`) and work the same in every launch phase (`L1-021`).

## L1-024: Showcase listings and item requests
TuTuMuch's owner, signed in as an administrator (`L1-002`), shall be able to mark one of their own active listings as a showcase listing. A showcase listing shall look and browse like any active listing (`L1-005`, `L1-006`), but it is never reserved, sold or paid for (`L1-007`): choosing "Buy now" shows that the item has already found a home and offers "Request this item". A shopper shall make that request signed in, after signing in or joining and coming straight back to it; the owner shall see every request in the admin shell. Requests are private to the member and the owner (`L1-014`) and work the same in every launch phase (`L1-021`).
